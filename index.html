Hashomer Hatzair & Soviet Alignment

Created in 1941 in Mandatory Palestine, this event graphic employs Soviet constructivist style (specifically referencing Vera Mukhina’s landmark Worker and Kolkhoz Woman motif). It documents early Left-Zionist ideological alignment with Soviet socialist realism prior to the USSR's official anti-Zionist turn post-1967.

Origin / Date 1941 / Mandatory Palestine
Publisher Hashomer Hatzair Press

DFLP Armed Struggle Messaging

Published by the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine (DFLP), this print incorporates high-contrast woodcut illustration with Arabic verse ("Men carry weapons and continue the path"). It demonstrates how 20th-century Arab political movements adapted Third-Worldist revolutionary aesthetics.

Origin / Date c. 1970s / Regional Arab Press
Organization DFLP Media Division

Western Anti-Imperialist Satire

Illustrated by American political cartoonist Dwayne Booth (Mr. Fish), this graphic represents modern Western grassroots anti-Zionist commentary. The piece utilizes stark editorial caricature to link American cultural holidays directly to foreign policy critique.

Artist Mr. Fish (Dwayne Booth)
Medium Editorial Satirical Print
